# Community Energy Resilience Platform

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The project team spans business, software and electronics disciplines. Reviewers use the app to understand and operate the engineering demonstrator for Ban Sala Hum. Community stakeholders and field validation are described in the source documents; actual users and installation sites still require confirmation.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate P0–P3 energy prioritization, monitoring, fault classification and low-voltage circuit relationships using a browser-local deterministic simulator.

## Operating Context

Hackathon team planning, engineering discussion and guided presentation. Source evidence is the community Data Package and team summary. The app has eleven content routes and runs as a static Next.js export on the existing GitHub Pages site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Interactive village and circuit scenes, six scenario controls, charts, event history, JSON/CSV exports and technical documentation. Preserve electrical simulation equations and priority P0 > P1 > P2 > P3. Telemetry offline, electrical outage and sensor fault are separate. DO is unavailable. Demonstrator loads, battery capacity and sensor values are simulated. Scene geometry and proposed equipment must not be represented as surveyed or installed infrastructure.

## Brand Commitments

User-requested Cinematic Coastal Industrial Digital Twin: premium dark navy control center, marine teal accents, minimalist readable typography, realistic materials, local field references, cinematic outage feedback and complete mobile/desktop operation.

## Evidence on Hand

`docs/source-of-truth.md`, `docs/engineering-notes.md`, source Data Package and team summary. Published field photos in the parent repository. Asset provenance and licenses are recorded in `ASSET_LICENSES.md`. Field performance and precise layout are not established.

## Product Principles

- Keep values and reconstructed geometry visibly labelled.
- Let the shared simulator drive every view.
- Preserve access to the full technical content and exports.
- Use local assets, bounded GPU cost and responsive controls.
