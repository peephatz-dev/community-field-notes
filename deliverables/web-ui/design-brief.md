# Isometric dashboard direction

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site itself is the work surface. A visitor selects equipment and sees the same electrical event in the scene, inspector and history.
OWN-WORLD: Existing white/sky surfaces, navy Thai type and exact PowerGuard palette. Retain the real SVG fish mascot and local coastal geometry/materials.
STORY: See where equipment is proposed, inspect a load, simulate a fault, understand which equipment remains powered, acknowledge and record the response.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full main-area 3D scene at desktop, four compact floating indicators upper left, equipment inspector upper right, camera controls and incident sequence along the bottom. Small mascot below the inspector. Charts and evidence continue beneath the scene. Mobile stacks inspector and controls with a reachable 2D fallback.
FORM: User-supplied WareTrack composition applied to the existing coastal digital twin. The source image is a private composition reference, not a site asset. seed: none (unavailable).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Shared constraints
Three audience modes: ชาวบ้าน, ผู้ดูแล, วิศวกร. The first two use equipment names; engineering shows P0–P3. All current data is simulated. Sound stays opt-in. The model, routes, source image geometry, quality boundaries and existing exports remain.

User confirmed exactly ten 16:9 slides and six minutes. Problem, Innovation, Feasibility, Impact and Presentation are preparation criteria, not a verified official rubric. Hardware and field impact remain unverified unless supported by repository evidence.
